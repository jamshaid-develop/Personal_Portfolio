The publisher wanted something between a scientific field guide and a coffee table book — precise enough for a marine biologist, warm enough for a Sunday read.

I shot roughly 1,800 reference photos across low tides throughout the year and edited the final 340 that made the book. Layout followed a strict baseline grid, broken deliberately whenever a full-bleed photograph demanded the page.
