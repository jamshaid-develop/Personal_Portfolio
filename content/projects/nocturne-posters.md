The Nocturne series runs every new moon — chamber music performed after midnight in a converted textile mill. I designed one poster per show, all sharing a type system but never a layout.

Each poster was risograph-printed in-house on a two-drum Ricoh, which meant designing for registration drift rather than against it. Misalignment became the visual signature of the whole series.
