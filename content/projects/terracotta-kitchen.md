Terracotta Kitchen came to the studio with forty SKUs and no shelf presence. The brief was simple: make it feel like it was dug out of the ground, not printed in a factory.

We built the identity around a custom stamp mark — literally pressed into wet clay and scanned — paired with a restrained two-color print system on uncoated stock. Every box unfolds into a care card, so nothing gets thrown away.

Six months post-launch, wholesale orders tripled and the packaging now ships to eleven countries.
