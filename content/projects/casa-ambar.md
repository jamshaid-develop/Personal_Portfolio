Casa Ámbar sits on a ridge that catches the last light of the day for exactly forty minutes — the owners built the whole guest experience around it, so the brand had to too.

The identity uses a single warm amber that shifts saturation depending on print stock, echoing that changing light. Signage is hand-routed cedar; the website is a single scrolling page timed to a real sunset.
